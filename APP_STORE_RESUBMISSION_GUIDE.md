Use the current release sources instead:

- `APP_STORE_SETUP_CHECKLIST.md` for the version 1.3 submission gates.
- `SUBSCRIPTION_SETUP_GUIDE.md` for current Solo, Team, and legacy product handling.
- `docs/team-billing-rollout.md` for the server-authoritative Team entitlement rollout.
- `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`, `TERMS_OF_USE.md`, and `SUPPORT.md` for the current customer-facing text.

The legacy weekly and yearly products remain recognized for existing subscribers. New purchases use the Solo and Team product identifiers documented in `SUBSCRIPTION_SETUP_GUIDE.md`.
